Madobe · Help and contact

Madobe is a small web browser for Mac, iPhone and iPad, built on WebKit. If something is broken or unclear, write to me and I will read it.

Contact

trommatic@icloud.com. Tell me your device, the system version and the address of the page if it is about one site.

Common questions

How do I save a page? Tap the star next to the address. Open the ellipsis menu, then Bookmarks, to see and search your saved pages. On the Mac, use the Bookmarks menu or Command-D.

How do I see or clear my history? Ellipsis menu, then History. Swipe a row to delete it, or choose Clear. Settings also has Clear History. On the Mac, use the History menu.

What is a private tab? Ellipsis menu, then New Private Tab. It keeps no history, cookies or cache, and it is not restored when you reopen the app. Private tabs show an eyeglasses icon.

Can I turn off tracker and ad blocking? Yes: Settings, Privacy, or the ellipsis menu. If a site misbehaves, turn it off, reload, and tell me which site it was.

How do I change the search engine or homepage? Settings, Search. You can choose DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Ecosia, Startpage or Google.

How do I find text on a page? Ellipsis menu, then Find in Page, or Command-F on the Mac.

A page will not load. Check your connection, then reload. If a page says it stopped responding, reload it. If it keeps happening, email me the address.

How your data is handled is in the privacy policy.
