Privacy Policy

Janela · Last updated 8 September 2026

Janela is a web browser. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you is collected, stored or sold by us. The app never asks who you are.

What leaves your device

Only the addresses you type and the pages you visit, sent directly to those websites. If you type something that is not an address, it is sent to DuckDuckGo as a search. Nothing is relayed through any server of ours.

What is kept

Nothing on any server. Cookies, cache and site data are kept on your device by WebKit, the same engine Safari uses, and are removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

Every website you open receives your request and handles it under its own privacy policy. DuckDuckGo handles searches under its own policy. We have no relationship with any of them.

Children

Janela does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it.
